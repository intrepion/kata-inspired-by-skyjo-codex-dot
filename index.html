THE RULES

Small numbers win.

Reveal two cards to begin. The highest opening sum starts the first round; the finisher starts each later round.

On your turn, take the discard and swap it with any card, or draw blindly and either swap it or discard it and reveal a hidden card. Three equal face-up cards in a vertical column are discarded.

Once a player has no hidden cards, every other player takes one final turn. Reveal and clear matching columns, then add the remaining values. If the finisher has a positive score that is tied or beaten by another player, their round score doubles. Play until someone reaches 100; the lowest total wins.

Opening ties go to the first player in seating order. If the draw pile runs out, the discard pile is shuffled except for its top card.
